01 · Background

Three stacked layers compose every page: the flat --bg-base fill, an animated dot-field canvas (z-index:-1), and a fixed fractal-noise grain overlay (z-index:9999). The dot-field running behind this whole page is the real component — a 14px grid where a Perlin/fBm noise mask reveals clusters of 1px dots, and short dashes "hop" between neighbouring cells.

02 · Gradients & glow

All gradients are accent-driven and derive from --accent-tint (8% alpha), --accent-glow (35% dark / 18% light), and --accent-bright. They wash sections, separators, and hover states. Cycle the accent to see them shift in hue.

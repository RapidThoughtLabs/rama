The heku console reads like a terminal that grew a UI — monospace throughout, near-black canvas, one hot accent hue, and a hardware-style glow. This document is the complete, live token set and component vocabulary. Change the mode and accent above and the whole page re-themes: that runtime themeability is the system. Everything here is copy-ready CSS you can lift into a new product.

01 — Philosophy

Decisions, not just tokens

Five convictions drive every choice below. Port these first; the specific hex values matter less than the reasoning.

P1

Monospace as identity

One typeface family (JetBrains Mono) for everything — body, labels, numbers, buttons. DM Mono is reserved only for the display wordmark. The uniform grid signals "tool for builders," keeps numeric data aligned, and removes an entire axis of decision-making.

P2

One accent, runtime-swappable

The accent is stored as three HSL parts, never a hex. Tints, dims, and glows all derive from it, so a single hue change re-skins the product. Six presets ship; the mechanism supports infinite.

P3

Dark-first, warm neutrals

The baseline is near-black #0a0a0a with warm off-white text #e8e8e0 — never pure black on pure white. Light mode is warm paper, not clinical white. Surfaces step up in four discrete tiers instead of using shadows to imply depth.

P4

The halogen glow

Emphasis is expressed with a three-layer accent glow — a tight core, a mid bloom, a wide haze — evoking lit hardware. It appears on primary hover, active brand, and focus, and is the single most recognisable signature of the system.

P5

Dense, calm, flat

13px base, tight radii (4–10px), hairline #242424 dividers, and generous letter-spacing on labels. No drop shadows for elevation, no gradients, no decoration. Motion is quick (0.12–0.22s) and purposeful. The interface recedes so content leads.

P6

Traffic-light semantics

Status colors borrow the macOS window controls — red, yellow, green, blue. Instantly legible, culturally pre-loaded, and distinct from the themeable accent so meaning never shifts when the accent does.

04 — Typography

One family, tuned by tracking

JetBrains Mono carries the entire UI; DM Mono is display-only (wordmarks, headings). Because everything is monospace, hierarchy comes from size, weight, and letter-spacing rather than typeface contrast. Note the rule: small uppercase labels get wide positive tracking (0.14–0.20em); large display text gets tight negative tracking (−0.02 to −0.04em).

Weights & rhythm

Base is 13px on :root (user-adjustable 11–18px); all sizes are rem so a single root change rescales the app. Weights used: 400 regular · 500 medium (display) · 600 semibold (names, labels) · 700 bold (primary buttons, titles). Line-height sits at 1.5–1.6 for body, 1–1.02 for display.

05 — Space, radius & elevation

Tight and deliberate

Spacing follows a compact scale — 2 · 4 · 6 · 8 · 10 · 12 · 14 · 18 · 20 · 22px. Radii are role-based, not a single global value. Depth is drawn with borders and surface steps, never ambient shadow — the only shadows in the system are modal lift and the accent glow.

The halogen glow (elevation = light, not shadow)

rtl://

The glow is a stacked triple box-shadow: a 2px hot core at 70% alpha, a 10px bloom at 35%, and a 28px haze at 15% — all derived from the accent HSL. The wordmark hover uses the equivalent as a triple text-shadow. A red-tinted --cancel-glow mirrors it for destructive emphasis.

06 — Components

The vocabulary

Every interactive element is one of a small, fixed set. All are pill or small-radius, monospace, and transition all 0.15s. Hover reveals the accent; primary hover ignites the glow.

Buttons

Primary = solid accent pill, 700 weight, glow on hover. Ghost = outlined pill that fills with --accent-dim and adopts the accent border/text on hover. Cancel mirrors ghost in the fixed red. Icon = 4px square. Link = underlined accent text.

07 — Layout & navigation

The app shell

A fixed three-column frame: a 220px sidebar, a fluid main column, and an optional right panel. The whole app is 100vh, overflow: hidden — it never page-scrolls; regions scroll internally. Columns are separated by 1px --border dividers, not gaps.

Active nav = accent text + --accent-dim fill + a 2px accent left-border. Hover on inactive items lifts to --surface2 with --text-mid. Every row is icon + label, tracked at 0.06em.

Brand treatment

The wordmark is the load-bearing brand device: a dim rtl:// top-level-domain prefix (accent-colored rtl), then the product name in DM Mono with a single letter tinted the accent (heku). On hover the prefix ignites the neon text-shadow glow. This "protocol://product" motif is the core identity — carry it into the new product with its own name and highlighted letter.

08 — Motion

Quick, functional, looping

Transitions are 0.12–0.22s; entrances use the spring cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1). A handful of named keyframes cover live/loading states. Motion communicates state — it never decorates.

09 — Porting checklist

Taking this to a new product

To carry the design language forward without cloning the product, do these in order.

  • Copy the :root token block, the six [data-accent] presets, and the [data-mode="light"] overrides verbatim — they are the entire foundation.
  • Load JetBrains Mono (UI) and DM Mono (display), set the universal * { font-family } rule and 13px root.
  • Pick a default accent for the new product (or keep Plasma). Everything else recomputes from --accent-h/s/l.
  • Re-brand the wordmark: keep the protocol://product structure and the single accent-tinted letter; change the name.
  • Reuse the component classes as-is — buttons, badges, cards, seg control, toggle, modal, toast, nav item.
  • Preserve the signatures: the 3-layer --glow, the 2px accent stripe on cards/modals, hairline dividers, traffic-light semantics, and full-height no-page-scroll shell.
  • Wire the theme switch: set data-mode and data-accent on <html>, persist to localStorage. That's the whole theming engine.
  • Keep motion restrained — 0.15s default transitions, the spring easing for entrances, named keyframes only for genuine live/loading states.
